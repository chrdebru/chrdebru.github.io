[Frankie onder de mensen](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/250523341-frankie-onder-de-mensen) by [Jochen-Martin Gutch](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jochen-Martin_Gutsch) was an enjoyable read. From the cover and plot summary, you might think it's for children, but some themes are quite harsh and dark. I also appreciated the social criticism sprinkled across the book.

My rating: ⭐⭐⭐
