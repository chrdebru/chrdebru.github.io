I stumbled upon a second-hand copy of a Dutch translation of [Ira Levin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ira_Levin)'s [The Boys from Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Boys_from_Brazil_(novel)). I always wanted to read this book but never actively sought it out. I thought this was an excellent opportunity to get in some light Dutch reading during my holidays at the Belgian coast. I devoured the book. It was really good.

My rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
