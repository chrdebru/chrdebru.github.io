[The Leather Boys](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/241771894-the-leather-boys) by [Gillian Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gillian_Freeman) was a book I stumbled upon in this awesome bookstore in Ghent, the "Paard van Troje." I knew this book was an important part of old queer English literature, so I didn't hesitate to buy it. I must admit I left the place with various books... That book smacked me in the face. I was only vaguely aware of the premise, but not how it ended. Darn, writing about it gives me goosebumps. The dialogue and emotions were raw, and everything was so seemingly effortlessly tied together with the author's beautiful writing. It was a real page-turner.

My rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
