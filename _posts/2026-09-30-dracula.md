[Dracula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dracula) by [Bram Stoker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bram_Stoker) is one of the few gothic horror novels I didn't read in high school, when we covered the genre during our Germanic languages literature courses. I stumbled upon this book while in Dublin and decided I needed to read it. I also wanted to know what elements of the book were included in the [Curse of Strahd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curse_of_Strahd) D&D campaign. I was unaware (or I forgot) of how the book was written! I really enjoyed how the book is structured around written accounts from various people. It was an enjoyable and exciting read!

My rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
