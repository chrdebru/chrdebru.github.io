I haven't reported on any books in June! 😱 While I was reading [La Mémoire des Os -- Livre 1 : Le Dragon et l'Oiseau](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/218728213-le-dragon-et-l-oiseau) during the month of June, that month was mostly spent on reading MSc and MEng theses, grading papers and exams, ... The typical end-of-the-academic-year madness.


I finished [La Mémoire des Os -- Livre 1 : Le Dragon et l'Oiseau](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/218728213-le-dragon-et-l-oiseau) by [Margot Looten](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/51928665.Margot_Looten). From what I understand, this is her first book (666 pages, by the way) and she immediately aimed for a trilogy... The ambition. I took note of some inconsistencies in the book, such as some weird dialogues where information is all of a sudden introduced, a character appearing and reappearing, etc. But then again, this is her first book, and even Tolkien's books have some inconsistencies. By setting aside my "frustrations" for these inconsistencies, I must admit that I really enjoyed this fantasy cook.

My rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
